Who We Are

Good Samaritan Christian Foundation is a faith-based organization dedicated to helping those in need — the poor, the vulnerable, the orphaned, and the forgotten — just as Jesus taught us through the parable of the Good Samaritan.

We believe that every person deserves dignity, hope, and love.

"But a Samaritan, as he traveled, came where he was; and when he saw him, he took pity on him." — Luke 10:33

Our Mission

✅ To show God's love through practical compassion

✅ To empower families through education and resources

✅ To care for the sick, the hungry, and those in distress

✅ To unite people in kindness and faith
